Why we exist

Every circuit, every line of code, every system we build reaches into someone's life. We are students of engineering who believe that makes technology a responsibility, not just a skill. So we build with intent — to make the future smarter, and to make it fairer, safer and more human.

02 — The Global Community

Advancing technology for humanity.

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Its engineers, scientists, researchers, educators and students form a global community that publishes research, develops standards and convenes the conferences that move whole industries forward.

Visit ieee.org ↗
ChennaiVel Tech New YorkIEEE HQ

Lineage

  1. 1884The American Institute of Electrical Engineers (AIEE) is founded.
  2. 1912The Institute of Radio Engineers (IRE) is founded.
  3. 1963AIEE and IRE merge to form IEEE.
  4. TodayA worldwide community spanning every field of electrical, electronic and computing technology.

Standards

SSIT

Society on Social Implications of Technology

03 — Our Society

Technology is never neutral.

The IEEE Society on Social Implications of Technology studies how technology affects people, communities and the planet — and works to make sure engineering serves humanity. Its members explore ethics, sustainability, equity and the human consequences of the systems we build.

1972
Began as IEEE's Committee on Social Implications of Technology
1982
Became a full IEEE Society
Publishes
IEEE Technology and Society Magazine · IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society
Convenes
ISTAS — the IEEE International Symposium on Technology and Society
Visit technologyandsociety.org ↗

What SSIT stands for

The five pillars

01

Sustainable Development

Technology that meets today's needs without borrowing from tomorrow — clean energy, resilient infrastructure and responsible use of resources.

02

Ethics & Human Values

Asking not only whether we can build something, but whether we should — and how to build it right.

03

Universal Access to Technology

Closing digital divides so that the benefits of technology reach everyone, everywhere.

04

Societal Impacts

Understanding how systems reshape work, privacy, health and public life — before they ship, and after.

05

Protecting the Planet

Engineering in service of the environment — from climate technology to cutting electronic waste.

The chapter at Vel Tech

Building technology with its consequences in mind.

Mission

To grow a student community at Vel Tech that designs technology with people, society and the planet in view — from the first sketch to the final build.

Student Community

An open space for students across disciplines to learn, debate and build together, because responsible technology needs more than one perspective.

Leadership

Student-led and faculty-guided. Members plan, run and learn from every event — leadership is something we practise, not just a title.

Responsible Innovation

Every idea we take on meets the same questions: who does it serve, who could it harm, and how do we make it better for both?
